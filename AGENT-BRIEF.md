# Agent brief — Consumer legal-AI enforcement, UPL & authorisation register (read AGENT-BRIEF-COMMON.md first, then `schema/actions.schema.json`)

**Inclusion gate.** An entity appears only through an **official action or register entry**: regulator enforcement (FTC, state AGs, CFPB, CMA/ASA, SRA/LSB, law societies, consumer regulators), UPL litigation and bar/court UPL opinions naming AI services, regulator authorisations and sandbox entries **where the regulator's own text describes the service as AI/chatbot/software-based** (`regulator_ai_flag: true`; otherwise omit), court and access-to-justice AI deployments offered to self-represented people, and regulator policy statements that cite a legal-AI matter. This register never reviews, rates or recommends a service; no "legit" language; allegations exactly as the regulator framed them ("the complaint alleges…") paired with the outcome and any "did not admit liability" statement.

**Universe to verify and extend (all dates must be read from the primary source).** FTC *In re DoNotPay* — proposed consent order 2024-09-30 (89 FR 79594, File No. 232 3042; Federal Register API `https://www.federalregister.gov/api/v1/documents.json?conditions[term]=DoNotPay`), final Decision & Order Docket C-4812 (Jan 2025, $193,000), and the FTC's 2026-07-07 proposed policy statement citing it — ftc.gov blocks bots: use federalregister.gov, govinfo, and `web.archive.org` copies of the FTC matter page. *Faridian v. DoNotPay* (N.D. Cal. 3:23-cv-01692) and *MillerKing v. DoNotPay* (S.D. Ill. 3:23-cv-00863) — docket facts from RECAP PDFs/court sites; *Upsolve v. James* (2d Cir. No. 22-1345, 2025-09-09; remand status); BGH I ZR 113/20 *smartlaw* (2021-09-09, bundesgerichtshof.de press release; §5 UrhG). Registers: Utah Office of Legal Services Innovation authorized entities (`https://utahinnovationoffice.org/wp-json/wp/v2/pages?slug=authorized-entities` — one row per authorised/provisional/waiver entity whose description mentions AI/chatbot/software, each with its Utah Supreme Court order PDF as `source_url`; sunset 2027-08-14); SRA Garfield.Law authorisation (2025-05-06) and any later SRA AI-firm authorisations or decisions; Law Society of Ontario Access to Innovation participating providers; Law Society of BC Innovation Sandbox participants; Arizona ABS directory (azcourts.gov 403s bots — Wayback; include only entities the Court's own order/press describes as AI). Search for anything new in 2025–2026: state-AG actions on AI legal advice, CFPB, UK CMA/ASA rulings on "AI lawyer" adverts, Australian/NZ/Singapore/Indian regulator actions on AI legal-advice apps, state-bar UPL opinions naming AI tools (Florida, Texas, NC, California), Nevada/Arizona court self-help GenAI chatbots, court-run AI pilots for the public.
