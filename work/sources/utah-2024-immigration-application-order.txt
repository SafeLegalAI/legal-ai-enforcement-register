    IN THE SUPREME COURT OF THE STATE OF UTAH




                            ----oo0oo----




ORDER DISCONTINUING ACCEPTANCE OF APPLICATIONS
TO THE SANDBOX FROM FOR-PROFIT ENTITIES PRIMARILY
     OFFERING IMMIGRATION RELATED SERVICES


The Utah Supreme Court discontinues acceptance of applications to
the Sandbox from for-profit ABS entities solely or primarily offering
immigration-related services.




DATED this 16th day of September 2024.
