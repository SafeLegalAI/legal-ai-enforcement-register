       In re: AAA Fair Credit Foundation/NeighborWorks Salt Lake

           ORDER TERMINATING AUTHORIZATION TO PRACTICE LAW

Based upon the Utah Supreme Court’s plenary and constitutionally granted
authority to regulate the practice of law in Utah, and the tenets of Standing
Order 15, the Utah Supreme Court orders that AAA Fair Credit
Foundation/NeighborWorks Salt Lake’s authorization is terminated.

The Court has reviewed the Request to Withdraw submitted by AAA Fair Credit
Foundation/NeighborWorks Salt Lake.

In light of the Court’s responsibility to the public to effectively regulate the
practice of law in Utah and in keeping with the tenets of Standing Order 15, the
Court now orders as follows:

Any and all orders allowing AAA Fair Credit Foundation/NeighborWorks Salt Lake
and People’s Legal Aid to practice law within the Utah legal regulatory sandbox
(the “Sandbox”) are terminated. This includes the:

   •    September 21, 2022 Amended Order for Authorization to Practice Law in re
        Application of AAA Fair Credit Foundation
   •    May 6, 2021 Order for Authorization to Practice Law in re Application of
        AAA Fair Credit Foundation/People’s Legal Aid

In the event these entities, or any associated entities, wish to practice law within
the Sandbox, they are required to seek a new authorization order through the
application process.


DATED this 15th day of November, 2023.
