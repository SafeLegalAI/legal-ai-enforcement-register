                      ORDER TERMINATING AUTHORIZATION TO PRACTICE LAW


        Based upon the Utah Supreme Court’s plenary and constitutionally granted
        authority to regulate the practice of law in Utah, and the tenets of Standing Order
        15, the Utah Supreme Court orders that Rocket Lawyer Inc./Rocket Legal
        Professional Services, Inc.’s (“Rocket Lawyer/Rocket Legal”) authorization is
        terminated, effective December 20, 2024.1

        The Legal Services Innovation Committee has approved Rocket Lawyer/Rocket
        Legal’s Request to Withdraw, effective December 20, 2024.

        In light of the Court’s responsibility to the public to effectively regulate the practice
        of law in Utah, and in keeping with the tenets of Standing Order 15, the Court now
        orders as follows:

        Any and all orders authorizing Rocket Lawyer/Rocket Legal to provide services
        within the Utah legal regulatory sandbox (the “Sandbox”) are terminated as of
        December 20, 2024. This includes, but is not limited to:

             •   Amended Order for Authorization to Practice Law dated August 10, 2022
             •   Amended Order for Authorization to Practice Law dated September 21,
                 2020
             •   Order for Authorization to Practice Law dated August 31, 2020

        In the event this entity, or any associated entity, wishes to operate within the
        Sandbox, it is required to seek a new authorization order through the application
        process.

        DATED this 18th day of December, 2024.


                                                    ____________________________________
                                                    Matthew B. Durrant
                                                    Chief Justice

1
