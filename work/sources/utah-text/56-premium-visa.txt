            ORDER TERMINATING AUTHORIZATION TO PRACTICE LAW

Based upon the Utah Supreme Court’s plenary and constitutionally granted
authority to regulate the practice of law in Utah, and the tenets of Standing Order
No. 15, the Utah Supreme Court orders that Premium Visa, LLC's authorization is
terminated.

The Court has reviewed the Request to Withdraw submitted by Premium Visa, LLC.

In light of the Court’s responsibility to the public to effectively regulate the practice
of law in Utah, and in keeping with the tenets of Standing Order No. 15, the Court
now orders as follows:

Any and all orders authorizing Premium Visa, LLC to provide services within the
Utah legal regulatory sandbox (the “Sandbox”) are terminated. This includes the
following:
    • April 10, 2023 Order for Authorization to Practice Law in Utah

In the event this entity, or any associated entity, wishes to operate within the
Sandbox, it is required to seek a new authorization order through the application
process.
