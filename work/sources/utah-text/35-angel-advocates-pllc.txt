            ORDER TERMINATING AUTHORIZATION TO PRACTICE LAW

Based upon the Utah Supreme Court’s plenary and constitutionally granted
authority to regulate the practice of law in Utah, and the tenets of Standing Order
No. 15, the Utah Supreme Court orders that Angel Advocates, PLLC's authorization
is terminated.

The Court has reviewed the Request to Withdraw submitted by Angel Advocates,
PLLC.

In light of the Court’s responsibility to the public to effectively regulate the practice
of law in Utah, and in keeping with the tenets of Standing Order 15, the Court now
orders as follows:

Any and all orders authorizing Angel Advocates, PLLC to provide services within
the Utah legal regulatory sandbox (the “Sandbox”) are terminated. This includes
the following:
    • October 12, 2022 Order for Authorization to Practice Law

In the event this entity, or any associated entity, wishes to operate within the
Sandbox, it is required to seek a new authorization order through the application
process.
